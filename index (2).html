Self-directed project

Audi Formula 1 fan engagement campaign

September 2026 – presentMarket researchCampaign strategy

Audi enters Formula 1 with its first season on the grid, which means it has to win fans from scratch. This project looks at how the brand could build awareness and lasting loyalty among college students, an audience discovering the sport through social media and streaming.

The research

Market and competitor research to find where Audi F1 can stand out against established teams with decades of fan history, and what actually gets college students to pick a team.

The strategy

A multi-channel campaign across social media, experiential activations, and partnerships, built so each channel moves students one step closer from noticing the team to calling it theirs.

How success is measured

AwarenessDo students know Audi is on the grid?
EngagementAre they interacting with the content?
Fan conversionAre they choosing Audi as their team?

Marketing research class project

Why are Truly Unruly sales slipping?

Fall 2026 – presentMKTG 3401, NortheasternClient: The Boston Beer Company

For my marketing research class, our group of five is working with The Boston Beer Company on a real business problem. Truly Unruly, the brand's 8% hard seltzer line, grew steadily for years, then its sales started to fall. Our job is to turn that challenge into research the brand can act on.

The management decision problem

Why are sales of Truly Unruly declining in 2026 despite consistent growth in previous years?

Five research problems

We split the question into five areas, with each teammate leading one.

Packaging, my focusHow different packaging designs and sizes are perceived by the target market
PriceWhat shapes perceptions of price and value for ready-to-drink alcohol
ABVHow much the target market values alcohol content relative to other attributes
Brand awarenessHow consumers perceive Truly Unruly's recognition, appeal, and value
Brand loyaltyWhich hard seltzer brands consumers prefer, and how loyal they are

My part: packaging

I'm researching how pack size, convenience, and visual design influence what people buy and whether they'll try a new drink. Early secondary research suggests packaging could be shaping both first purchases and repeat buying, so my survey questions are designed to test which formats and designs resonate with our target market.

How we're approaching it

So far we've defined the problem with our client, gathered secondary research from sources including Boston Beer's annual report, IWSR, and NielsenIQ, built a sampling and recruitment plan, and designed a Qualtrics survey with one block of questions per research problem. Next, we'll collect and analyze the data and present recommendations to Boston Beer.
